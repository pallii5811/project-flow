# First outreach email — licensors and producers

One email per company, written to a named team or contact form. Fill every bracket with
something specific to that company; an email that could go to anyone gets the answer anyone
gets. Never claim audience numbers: we have none until the closed beta.

---

**Subject:** Non-exclusive free window for your vertical dramas, 50% revenue share

Hi [team or name],

I'm Simone, founder of PROJECT FLOW (working name), a free web platform for vertical short
dramas. Viewers never pay, unlock or collect coins; revenue comes from sponsors and an ad load
capped by a written charter (no ads in the first 10 minutes, at most 3 minutes per viewing hour).

We are assembling our English launch catalog: 45–60 completed series in billionaire/CEO
romance, revenge & comeback, and werewolf/supernatural romance. I'm writing to you because
[specific reason — e.g. your catalogue lists titles with Western casts and offers
non-exclusive AVOD windows].

What we offer, per title:

- 50% of the ad and sponsor revenue of each market, split by verified minutes watched
- non-exclusive: you keep all rights, and set territories and windows
- no fees and no minimums; monthly statements that tie out to the cent

The full terms, including how minutes are counted and an example statement:
[link to the producer terms page]

We are pre-launch and have no audience numbers yet. A closed beta comes first, and its data is
shared with launch partners.

Could you send a list of titles available for a non-exclusive AVOD window in English-speaking
territories? We will reply with a title-by-title proposal.

Best regards,
Simone
[company name, website, business contact]
