The terms

What you get, title by title

Revenue share
50% of the ad and sponsor revenue of each market, split across series by verified minutes watched, every month.
Exclusivity
None. Keep selling your titles anywhere else.
Rights
Yours. We license a free, ad-supported window, nothing more.
Territories and windows
Set by you, per title. Territories, languages and dates travel with the title in our catalog, and the site refuses to list it outside its window: the check runs at every build, so a closed window takes it out at the next publish.
Fees and minimums
None on either side. One title or a full catalogue.
Statements
Monthly, per series and market. Totals tie out to the cent.

How minutes are counted

Only minutes that were really watched

The player reports its position every few seconds. Watched time is rebuilt from those reports with fixed rules, written as code and covered by tests. We can walk you through them.

  • PlayingCounts in full.
  • PausedEarns nothing, however long the pause.
  • Skipping aheadEarns only the real time that passed, not the distance jumped.
  • RewindingEarns again only once playback moves forward.
  • Resuming laterPaid from the point of resumption, not from the start.
  • Network retriesA report sent twice is counted once.

Fit

What we are looking for, and what we ask for

Titles

  • Completed English-language series, typically 60–100 episodes.
  • Billionaire and CEO romance, revenge and comeback, werewolf and supernatural romance.
  • Library titles welcome: a second life for series that already earned elsewhere.
  • Evidence of past performance helps a title join the launch selection.

Per title

  • Vertical masters at 1080×1920 or higher, one file per episode.
  • Subtitles in VTT or SRT.
  • Poster art, synopsis, episode list, genre and tropes.
  • Rights: territories, languages, window, producer of record.

On us

What we take care of

  • Encoding for every network: adaptive streaming in 2-second segments, up to four quality levels, as high as your master allows and never upscaled.
  • Sound levelled: every episode normalised to the same loudness (EBU R128, −16 LUFS), measured on the file we serve, so your title is never the quiet one in the feed.
  • Delivery at our cost, from a link that plays with no install and no sign-up.
  • Social clips — planned, not built yet. Your answer is recorded per title before anything exists, and it is no unless you say yes.
  • Statements you can check line by line.
